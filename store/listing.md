## Description

Clutch records your games and saves the moment you just played, instantly. Press F9 and the last 30 seconds are saved. Or let Clutch clip for you: it can catch hype moments when you or your friends get loud, save every stretch of conversation in voice chat, and, if you want, watch the screen for the moments you describe in plain English.

Your clips are yours. Clutch keeps everything on your PC. There's no account to make, no analytics and no tracking, and nothing is uploaded unless you choose to share it.

- Instant replay: an always-on buffer that saves the last moments with a hotkey, using GPU encoding so your game keeps its frame rate.
- Long recordings: record a whole session with Alt+F9.
- Clips that make themselves: hype detection and voice-chat triggers run entirely on your PC with no AI.
- Optional AI clipping: describe what to clip ("boss kills, goals, aces") and use your own Anthropic API key.
- A fast library: clips sorted by game, with search that forgives typos and understands "funny minecraft last week".
- Built-in editor: trim, crop to 9:16 for TikTok, Shorts and Reels, and add captions that are made on your PC.
- Share your way: copy clips into your Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox or iCloud folder, upload to YouTube, or post to Discord.
- Discord and voice chat on their own audio track, so you can mix later.
